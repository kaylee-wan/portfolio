Pride Month Event Poster

Poster Design Event Branding Bilingual Layout Community Building Apr – Jun 2024
Summary

In June 2024, Chinese Rainbow Network was preparing to march as a Chinese LGBTQ+ contingent in the San Francisco Pride Parade. Marching together works better when people already know each other, so CRN ran two in-person meetups earlier in the month. This poster was the invitation for both — one call, two dates, two cities.

The Poster
Two events, one frame

June 08 in San Francisco and June 16 at Quarry Lakes sit side by side at equal weight, split by a lighthouse — a landmark that belongs to both the bay and the idea of finding your way to people.

Bilingual by default

English carries the logistics; Chinese carries the invitation. CRN's audience reads both, and splitting the jobs kept the poster from feeling like a translation of itself.

Pride without cliché

The rainbow is a ribbon along the bottom rather than a background wash, so the dates stay the loudest thing on the page.

Rollout & Outcome

The poster ran on CRN's Instagram and across the community WeChat groups, where I also answered sign-up questions directly — what to expect, who else was coming, whether it was okay to show up alone. For a first-time attendee that conversation matters as much as the graphic; the poster gets attention, the reply gets someone to actually come. I worked with the promo team weekly and passed participant feedback back so later posts landed clearer.

What I Learned

A community poster isn't decoration — it's the first step of a funnel that ends with 170 people standing next to each other on Market Street. Designing it meant thinking about who feels invited, not just what looks good, and staying in the group chat long after the graphic shipped.
