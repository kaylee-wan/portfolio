Wayfair externship · Business & data analysis

Five agents for one rug category

I spent the externship building five n8n workflows around a single Wayfair category: shag rugs. They run from a moodboard generator through trend and competitor research to a dashboard a category manager could open in a meeting. Each one ends in a document, not a demo.

The category, the program brief and the starting workflows were given by the externship. Several of them did not run. I traced the failures to the prompts, found the nodes that were breaking, fixed them, and kept iterating until each workflow produced a usable output. Sample sizes are small on purpose (the competitor work compares ten products per retailer), so the findings are directional rather than market truth.
